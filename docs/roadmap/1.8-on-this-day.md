# 1.8 — On This Day

*Released as 1.8.0 on 2026-11-18, packages 1–3 and share cards. As built:*

- *`scripts/build-almanac.py` writes almanac.json from ids.json (exact births and deaths; a date with a disputed
  alternative is left out) and sessions.json (exact session dates of reviewed, non-compilation albums): 320 events on
  207 days. The validator fails if it's stale. Album release dates and milestone dates aren't in yet; they'd fill
  more of the 159 empty days.*
- *Lines are templates; a record made over several days says "began recording" or "finished recording" rather than
  claiming it was made in one day. Deaths rank last and read as remembrance.*
- *Share cards render with `ImageRenderer` at 1080 × 1350: a record's card has its cover; a day's card is
  typographic, so it never waits on a photo.*
- *The **widgets** came after, in 1.9: one extension (`Widgets/`) for both platforms, with **Today in Jazz** (small,
  medium, large; Lock Screen rectangular and inline on iPhone) and **Continue** (your path's next stop, or the stamp
  you're closest to; small, medium, Lock Screen). The extension carries only jazz.json, sessions.json, almanac.json and
  catalog.json, and fetches the day's cover or portrait (300 px) when it builds its timeline: today, then tomorrow from
  midnight. The app writes a `WidgetSnapshot` to the app group whenever the passport changes and reloads the widgets.
  The widget views live in `Shared/Design/WidgetViews.swift`, so the Mac app's `--snapshots <dir> --widgets-only`
  draws them for review. StandBy uses the small widget; there are no StandBy-specific layouts.*
- *1.10 brought the rest: App Intents (Play a Station, including place and label stations; Today in Jazz; Continue My
  Path; Play Next Stop; Open Artist / Record / Tune) with App Shortcuts phrases; artists, records and tunes in Spotlight
  (`IndexedEntity`, indexed once per data version); a play button on the Continue widget (an `AudioPlaybackIntent`,
  which runs in the app); and the morning note (`MorningNote`, Settings ▸ Today in Jazz, the next seven days
  scheduled locally).*

A reason to open the app tomorrow. Today's spotlight picks an artist by counting days and taking the remainder
(`SpotlightCard` in `HomeView.swift` and `TimelineTab.swift`), so it's arbitrary. With exact dates it can be true:
whose birthday it is, what was recorded on this date, what happened this week. The same content goes on the Home
Screen and Lock Screen as widgets, and out of the app as share cards.

Needs 1.1 for `ids.json` (birth and death dates) and `sessions.json` (recording dates).

## What people get

- **Today** at the top of Discover: a tear-off calendar page with what happened on this date, a portrait, and one tap
  to play.
- **This Week**: the next seven days.
- **The Almanac**: browse any date.
- **Widgets** on iPhone (Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy) and Mac (desktop, Notification Centre): Today, and your
  next path stop.
- **Share cards**: a poster image for a record, an artist, a day or a finished stamp.
- **Ask for it**: "Play bebop radio" and "What happened in jazz today" in Shortcuts and Siri; artists, records and
  tunes in Spotlight search.
- Optionally, a morning notification with the day's entry. Off unless you turn it on.

## Data

### Dates

| Event | From | Coverage to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Born, died | `ids.json` (1.1, from Wikidata; Miles Davis returned 1926-05-26 and 1991-09-28) | Nearly all artists. Early figures with uncertain dates have year precision only and are left out. |
| Recorded | `sessions.json` (1.1) | Albums with dated sessions; compilations excluded. |
| Released | A new optional `released` on albums, filled by `build-sessions.py` from the release group's first date when it has day precision | Partial; older records often have only a year. |
| Milestones | A new optional `date` on milestones in `jazz.json`, by hand, where the event has one (a concert, an opening night) | Perhaps half of the 40. |
| Tunes | First recordings of Songbook versions, when sessions data dates them | Partial. |

With about 130 artists (two dates each), 150 dated sessions and the rest, expect 450 to 550 events, so most days have
one and many have two or three. The validator prints a calendar heat map of empty days, which becomes a to-do list for
the catalogue.

### `almanac.json` (new, generated)

`scripts/build-almanac.py` flattens the dates above into one list keyed by month and day, so the app and the widget
don't recompute it:

```json
{
  "05-26": [
    { "kind": "born", "year": 1926, "artist": "miles-davis" }
  ],
  "03-02": [
    { "kind": "recorded", "year": 1959, "album": "kind-of-blue", "place": "CBS 30th Street Studio", "tracks": ["So What", "Blue in Green"] }
  ]
}
```

It's rebuilt by the script whenever its inputs change; the validator fails if it's stale.

### Sentences

Each event needs a line. They're generated from templates, not hand-written, with a few variants per kind so the
calendar doesn't read like a form: "Miles Davis was born in Alton, Illinois, 100 years ago today." / "On this day in
1959, the first *Kind of Blue* session, at Columbia's 30th Street Studio." Round anniversaries (25, 50,
75, 100) are called out. Birthplaces come from Wikidata with the dates, if we choose to fetch them. An optional
`"line"` on any event overrides the template when something better is worth writing.

## Shared code

### `Shared/Model/Almanac.swift`

```swift
struct DayEvent: Codable, Hashable, Identifiable { enum Kind { case born, died, recorded, released, milestone } … }
struct Almanac {
    func events(month: Int, day: Int) -> [DayEvent]
    func lead(for date: Date, library: JazzLibrary) -> DayEvent?        // the one to feature
    func upcoming(from date: Date, days: Int) -> [(Date, DayEvent)]
    func line(for event: DayEvent, on date: Date, library: JazzLibrary) -> String
    func play(for event: DayEvent, library: JazzLibrary) -> PlayRequest // the album, or the artist's signature tracks
}
```

Choosing the lead: round anniversaries first; then sessions and releases (they come with something specific to play);
then births; then deaths. Among equals, artists and records you've saved, then ones you haven't heard. On an empty
day, the nearest event within three days ("Tomorrow: …"), and failing that the existing rotating spotlight, so the
card is never blank.

Dates use the user's calendar day. February 29 events also show on February 28 in common years.

`Route.almanac(month:day:)`; `PlayContext.today`.

### Widgets

A new target, `JazzDiscoveryWidgets`, one extension built for both platforms.

- **Today** (small, medium, large; Lock Screen rectangular and inline; StandBy): the calendar page with the date, the
  line, and the duotone portrait or cover. Tapping opens the event's page (`jazzdiscovery://today`).
- **Continue** (small, medium): your current path and next stop, or your nearest unfinished era stamp.
- The timeline provider builds entries for the next several days in one go, since the content is all in the bundle.
  Images are fetched in the provider (Wikipedia thumbnail, album artwork) and stored in the shared container.
- The duotone look is `Duotone` in `Shared/Design/Images.swift`, which is plain SwiftUI (grayscale plus two blended
  overlays), so it should render in a widget; tinted and clear Home Screen modes flatten colours, so the widget needs
  a variant for those that relies on shape and type, not ink.

Project changes this needs:

- The new target in `project.pbxproj`, with `Shared/Model`, `Shared/Design` and `Shared/Resources` as members (the
  project uses synchronized folders; the widget gets membership of those folders, not `Shared/Services`).
- A second bundle ID (`io.github.davidlago.JazzDiscovery.Widgets`) and an **app group** so the app can hand the
  passport's state and cached images to the widget. `Passport` writes a small snapshot there.
- Signing: `scripts/provisioning-profiles.swift` already takes several bundle IDs; `testflight.sh` and the export
  options need the second profile. On the Mac, the Developer ID profile work from 1.2 extends to the extension, and
  the Mac app's sandbox gets the app group entitlement.
- **You**: register the app group and the widget's bundle ID in the developer portal (or let Xcode's automatic signing
  do it on the first local build).
- CI builds the extension as part of each app; no new jobs.

### Share cards

`Shared/Design/ShareCard.swift`: SwiftUI views rendered to an image with `ImageRenderer`, in the app's poster style,
1080 × 1350: the day's event; a record ("why it matters" and the cover); an artist; a completed stamp or path. Shared
through `ShareLink` with the image and a `jazzdiscovery://` link. Cards include only what's already public in the app;
a passport card shows a stamp, not a listening history. Images used on cards are Wikimedia Commons photos and Apple
Music artwork, as in the app; the card carries the same attribution line the pages do.

### Shortcuts, Siri, Spotlight

- App Intents: *Play Station* (parameter: a station), *Open Today in Jazz*, *Continue My Path*, *Open Artist / Record
  / Tune*. Playing from an intent is the user's own request, unlike a link from a web page, so it may start music.
- Core Spotlight: index artists, records, tunes, places and terms with their blurbs, so system search finds "Kind of
  Blue" and opens the app's page. Indexed once per data version.

### Notification (optional)

Local only. When turned on in Settings, the app schedules the next seven days' leads at a time you pick, and refreshes
them each time it opens. No push, no server. The permission prompt appears only when the switch is turned on.

- `events` and `lead` for fixed dates: a birthday, a session day, an empty day (falls back within three days, then to

## Risks

- **The widget target** touches the project file, signing on both platforms and CI in one go. It's package 4 by
  itself for that reason, and it builds on the Mac profile work from 1.2; if 1.2's Mac sync was deferred, that work
  lands here first.
- **Empty or thin days.** The fallback chain keeps the card full; the heat map shows where the catalogue should grow.
- **Sad days.** A feed of death anniversaries is grim. Deaths rank last and are phrased as remembrance; births and
  sessions lead.
- **Date precision.** Wikidata marks precision; anything less than a day is skipped. Disputed birth dates (there are
  famous ones in early jazz) get a `"line"` override that says so, or are left out.
- **Widget images** need the network at timeline-build time; the widget falls back to the typographic placeholder the
  app already uses for artists without photos.

## Done when

The common checklist, plus: the Today card and the widget agree with each other and with the artist's page for any
date you try; changing the system date to an empty day still shows a sensible card; a shared card opens the right page
when its link is tapped on the other device.
