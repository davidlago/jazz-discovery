# 1.5 — Places & Labels

*Released as 1.5.0 on 2026-11-18. As built:*

- *47 places (15 cities and regions, 6 districts, 17 venues, 7 studios, 2 festivals) and the 14 labels with two or
  more Crate records. Coordinates were set by hand and checked against Wikipedia by `build-places.py` (two
  corrected; eleven have no Wikipedia coordinates and keep the hand-set ones).*
- *`era.places` stays as text: era pages show the places whose `eras` include the era instead, so no breaking change
  to jazz.json was needed.*
- *Places join `sessions.json` by name (`sessionNames`) rather than MusicBrainz place IDs.*
- *Not done: map clustering (cities only when zoomed out instead), city and label stations, Group by Label in the
  Crate, places and labels in search, and map snapshots on place cards.*
- *The app's own `MapCamera` (Connections) is now `GraphCamera`, as planned.*

Two more ways into the same music: *where* it happened and *who put it out*. Era pages already name 40 places as plain
text (Minton's Playhouse, the Village Vanguard, Van Gelder Studio) and every album names its label (20 are on Blue
Note, 14 on Columbia, 6 on Impulse!). This release turns both into pages.

Better with 1.1, which says where and when each record was made.

## What people get

- **A map of jazz**: cities, clubs, studios and festivals as era-coloured pins, with a **time slider** from 1895 to
  now. Drag it and New Orleans lights up, then Chicago and Harlem, then 52nd Street, then the West Coast.
- **City pages**: the story, the rooms, who was there, the records made there, and a station that plays the city.
- **Venue and studio pages**: what it was, a photo, the records recorded there (live albums especially), whether it's
  still open, and *Open in Maps*.
- **Label pages**: the label's story, its sound, the people behind it, and its records as a wall of covers in
  catalogue order.
- Places on era pages and labels on album pages become links.

## Data

### `places.json` (new, hand-written; coordinates by script)

```json
{
  "places": [
    {
      "id": "village-vanguard",
      "name": "Village Vanguard",
      "kind": "venue",
      "city": "new-york",
      "wikipedia": "Village_Vanguard",
      "wikidata": "Q670623",
      "lat": 40.736, "lon": -74.0016,
      "from": 1935, "to": null,
      "summary": "One or two paragraphs.",
      "eras": ["hard-bop", "modal", "post-bop"],
      "artists": ["bill-evans", "john-coltrane", "sonny-rollins"],
      "mbPlace": "…"
    }
  ]
}
```

- `kind`: `city`, `district` (Storyville, 52nd Street, Harlem), `venue`, `studio`, `festival`.
- `city` is the parent place's ID; cities have none.
- `from` / `to` are the years the place mattered to the music (not when the building was built); `to: null` means
  still active. The time slider uses them.
- `mbPlace` joins a place to the recording places in `sessions.json` (1.1), which is how "Recorded here" is filled
  without hand-listing albums. Live albums are found the same way.

The seed is the 40 strings already in `era.places`, cleaned up (they mix levels: "New York", "Harlem, New York",
"Minton's Playhouse, Harlem"), plus every studio and venue that 1.1's sessions name at least twice, plus a few cities
the catalogue growth brings in (London, Copenhagen, Tokyo, Johannesburg, Addis Ababa, Havana, Rio de Janeiro). Around
70 places: roughly 20 cities and districts, 30 venues, 10 studios, 10 festivals.

`era.places` in `jazz.json` changes from names to place IDs. That's a breaking change to one field, made in the same
commit as the views that read it.

### `scripts/build-places.py`

For each place with a `wikipedia` title: fetch the Wikidata item, fill `wikidata`, `lat`, `lon` (checked today:
the Vanguard and Minton's both return coordinates), and report places with none, which get coordinates by hand.
Districts and demolished clubs often have no coordinates on Wikidata; expect to place a dozen manually.

### `labels.json` (new, hand-written)

```json
{
  "labels": [
    {
      "id": "blue-note",
      "name": "Blue Note",
      "matches": ["Blue Note", "Blue Note Records"],
      "founded": 1939,
      "city": "new-york",
      "color": "#1E6FB8",
      "wikipedia": "Blue_Note_Records",
      "summary": "The story, two paragraphs.",
      "sound": "What its records tend to sound like, one paragraph.",
      "look": "What its covers look like and why, one paragraph.",
      "people": [
        { "name": "Alfred Lion", "role": "Founder, producer" },
        { "name": "Rudy Van Gelder", "role": "Engineer", "place": "van-gelder-studio" }
      ],
      "eras": ["bebop", "hard-bop", "soul-jazz", "post-bop"]
    }
  ]
}
```

`matches` maps the free-text `album.label` values onto the label, so albums don't need editing. Labels with two or
more records get a page: 14 today (Blue Note, Columbia, Impulse!, Bluebird, Verve, Atlantic, ECM, Milestone, Decca,
Savoy, EmArcy, Capitol, Warner Bros., Mack Avenue), more as the catalogue grows. The rest stay plain text.

No logos: label names are set in the app's own type, and the visual is their covers, which the app already shows as
album artwork.

### Validator

Place IDs unique, `city` resolves and is a city, coordinates within range and not (0, 0), `from ≤ to`, every
`era.places` entry resolves, every place belongs to at least one era or has at least one record. Label `matches`
don't overlap; each label with a page has at least two albums.

## Shared code

```swift
struct Place: Codable, Identifiable, Hashable { … ; var coordinate: CLLocationCoordinate2D
                                                func active(in year: Int) -> Bool }
struct Label: Codable, Identifiable, Hashable { … ; var tint: Color }

extension JazzLibrary {
    var places: [Place];  func place(_ id: String) -> Place?
    func places(in cityID: String) -> [Place]
    func albums(recordedAt placeID: String) -> [Album]     // through sessions.json
    func artists(at placeID: String) -> [Artist]           // listed, plus leaders of records made there
    var labels: [Label];  func label(for album: Album) -> Label?
    func albums(on labelID: String) -> [Album]
    func regulars(on labelID: String) -> [(Artist, Int)]    // leaders and credited players, by number of records
}
```

`Route.place(String)`, `.label(String)`, `.map`; `Station.Kind.place(String)` and `.label(String)` (a city station
plays tracks from records made there and by artists listed there; a label station plays its records). Search gains
places and labels.

The map is MapKit's SwiftUI `Map`, which both platforms have and which needs no key. The app asks for no location
permission. One name to clear first: the iPhone app already has its own `MapCamera` (the Connections map's pan and
zoom state, in `ConnectionsMap.swift`), and MapKit has a type of the same name. Rename the app's to `GraphCamera`
before importing MapKit.

## Mac

- **Sidebar**: **Places** and **Labels** in Explore.
- **`PlacesView`** (new): the map fills the page.
  - Style: the standard map, muted and dark, so the app's colours carry. Points of interest off.
  - Pins: `PlacePin`, a small disc in the tint of the place's first era, larger for cities, with the name beside it at
    closer zooms. Cities show a count and absorb their venues until you zoom in.
  - **Time slider** along the bottom: the River's coloured era bands as the track, a year readout in display type. Pins
    fade in when `active(in:)` becomes true and out afterwards. A play button sweeps it over about 40 seconds. Off by
    default ("All years") so the first view shows everything.
  - A place list in a side panel, grouped by city, filtered by the slider; selecting one flies the map there.
  - Shortcuts in the toolbar: New Orleans, Chicago, Kansas City, New York, Los Angeles, Europe, The World.
- **`PlaceDetailView`** (new):
  - City: hero from the Wikipedia image in duotone; the story; **The Rooms** (venue and studio cards, each a small
    static map snapshot with the name); **Who Was Here** (artist cards); **Made Here** (album cards from sessions);
    a mini time strip showing which eras touched the city; *Play Radio*.
  - Venue or studio: photo, years ("1935 – still open"), summary, **Recorded Here**, the artists, a small map, *Open in
    Maps*, the Wikipedia attribution.
- **`LabelsView`** (new): label cards as record spines and centre labels in each label's colour, with its record
  count and years covered.
- **`LabelDetailView`** (new): name in display type on the label's colour; story, sound and look as three short
  columns; **The People** (liner-credit style, linking to studios); **The Wall** (every record's cover in release
  order, dense, no captions until hover); **Regulars** (who appears on the most of its records, from 1.1); *Play
  Radio*.
- **`EraDetailView`**: place chips become links, plus a small map of the era's places.
- **`AlbumDetailView`**: the label becomes a link; the *Recorded* line's studio becomes a link.
- **`CrateView`**: *Group by Label* alongside by decade.

## iPhone

- **Explore tab**: **Places** and **Labels** rows. The Places row is a live map snippet.
- **`PlacesPage`**: the map full screen under a glass header; the time slider sits above the tab bar; the place list
  is a sheet with detents (peek, half, full), the way Maps does it, with the selected place's card in the peek.
- **`PlaceDetailView`, `LabelsPage`, `LabelDetailView`**: one column. The Wall is three covers across.
- **Era, album and Crate changes** as on the Mac.
- **Links**: `jazzdiscovery://place/<id>`, `map`, `label/<id>`, `labels`.

## Privacy

MapKit loads map tiles from Apple; add Apple Maps to the list of services in `docs/privacy.md`. No location is
requested or used. *Open in Maps* hands a coordinate to the Maps app.

## Tests

- `active(in:)` at the boundaries and with `to: nil`.
- `albums(recordedAt:)` through a fixture `sessions.json`; a studio's records roll up to its city.
- `label(for:)`: every bundled album's label string either matches exactly one label or none.
- Era pages still decode with `places` as IDs (and fail validation, not at runtime, if one is a leftover name).

## Risks

- **Map styling.** MapKit's dark, muted style is close to the app's ink background but not the same, and there's no
  custom tile styling. If it clashes, the fallback is the app's own stylised map for the overview (cities as dots on
  a drawn outline), with MapKit only at venue level.
- **Offline.** Map tiles need the network; the place list and pages don't. The map page shows the list alone when
  tiles don't load.
- **Pins on top of each other** in Manhattan. Clustering by city at low zoom, and at high zoom the real distances are
  enough; 52nd Street's clubs are a district page rather than six pins.
- **Thin "Made Here" lists** where sessions data has no studio. The section hides when empty.
- **Dates of a place's importance** are judgement calls; summaries carry the nuance, the slider only needs decades.
